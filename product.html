What it does

One roof for the winery’s daily work.

SansarBay is a small ERP. CRM, inventory, customer accounts, reporting, and the wine stock sit in the same login, so you aren't buying a different product for each job.

The boards

  • ClientsMove a client from lead, to prospect, to quoted, to customer. Drag the restaurant across when the relationship changes.
  • QuotesA quote stays on its own board until it becomes an invoice.
  • DispatchA case moves from the queue, to in process, to in transit (by sea, air or road), and then to arrived. You can still see every invoice in a list.
  • Stock healthA board shows what is short, what needs a reorder, and what is fine.

The release

  • AllocationsCompare the bottles an account is entitled to with the bottles they have used, for each wine.
  • LibraryHold a vintage until its date, and then release it.
  • DepletionRecord how many bottles an account has sold through.
  • Price listsUse a cellar, club, or trade list, or a list for one account. Margin follows the vintage.
  • Sample poolTasting bottles stay off the shelf you sell. The cellar-door till sells the rest.
  • CatalogueEach wine keeps its vintage, variety, region, barcode, and bottle photo.
  • Excise and levyPrepare NZ excise returns and NZ Winegrowers levy reports.
  • WarehouseDraw your cellar layout, keep each wine in a bin, and move stock between your sheds.
  • CurrencyPrice a customer in NZ dollars or US dollars.

Shopify and Xero by file today

Shopify sends a file in. Xero takes a file out.

A website order arrives as a spreadsheet you import. The books leave as a spreadsheet you import into Xero. SansarBay doesn't need access to your Xero or Shopify logins. Live connections are in development.

Underneath

You get invoices, credits, purchases, payments, and the shelf count. A mixed case sits on the invoice. Build a finished wine from bottles, closures, and labels. You can bring in your opening wines, customers, prices, and counts.
