Boutique wine producers

Who gets the wine, what they pay, and where the case is.

SansarBay is a simple ERP built for small wineries. Customers, stock, what each account owes, the reports, and the wine release all live in one login. You don't need a separate CRM, a stock app, and a wine system. It's built in New Zealand for wineries anywhere.

First week

Bring the wine list and the customer list.

We load the wines, the customers, the prices, and the shelf counts. You are not rebuilding the business before the first demo.

Boards, not a pile of grids

Clients, quotes, and dispatch each have a board. Move a card as the work progresses, or switch to the full list when you need every row.

A release, not a bin

Allocations show how many bottles each account is entitled to and how many they've taken. A library hold keeps a vintage back until its date. Tasting bottles sit in the sample pool, off the shelf you sell.

Three prices, one wine

Cellar, club, and trade prices sit on the same bottle. Margin follows the vintage. A mixed case goes on the invoice as you pack it.

What you open on a release

These are the wine jobs a warehouse system doesn't cover.

Small vintages are often promised before they're bottled. SansarBay is built for release week.

  • AllocationsSee how many bottles an account is entitled to, and how many they have used.
  • LibraryA vintage stays on hold until its release date, and then it is ready to sell.
  • DepletionSee how many bottles that account has sold through, wine by wine.
  • Cellar doorThe cellar door is a short till for a visitor. The sample pool stays separate from the shelf.
  • Price listsChoose cellar, club, trade, or a list for one account. The invoice uses the right one.
  • Excise and levyPrepare NZ excise returns and NZ Winegrowers levy reports.
  • WarehouseDraw your cellar layout, keep each wine in a bin, and move stock between your sheds.
  • CurrencyPrice a customer in NZ dollars or US dollars.
