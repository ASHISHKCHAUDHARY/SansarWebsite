A smaller promise

If you need a full warehouse system, we're not the right fit.

The daily work stays in SansarBay: customers, stock, accounts, reports, and the wine. A few jobs still belong in another product. We'll say so in the first meeting.

Not a full warehouse and courier system.

A large setup with several warehouses, couriers, and sales channels needs a bigger system. SansarBay covers what a small winery uses every day: invoices, stock, and wine pricing.

Not the cellar.

Tanks, lots, and vineyard traceability stay with a winemaking production system. If that's the problem you're solving, start there. SansarBay begins when the wine is bottled and ready to sell.

Not the online shop.

Shopify keeps the checkout and the club website. SansarBay reads a file of those orders so the shelf count can move. Live Shopify and Xero connections are coming.

Not your accounting system.

Your official books stay in Xero.
